# DOMAIN MODEL

Status: Approved

```text
Project
├── Contract
│   ├── Main Contract
│   ├── Work Packages
│   ├── Variations
│   ├── Omissions
│   └── Adjustments
├── Progress Measurements
├── Client Claim Cycles
│   ├── PCAR
│   ├── CCAR
│   ├── AR Invoice / Credit
│   └── Receipt / Allocation
├── Procurement
├── Subcontracts
│   ├── Claim
│   ├── Certification
│   └── AP / Payment
├── Cost Control
│   ├── Budget
│   ├── Committed
│   ├── Actual
│   └── Forecast / POC
└── Closeout
```

## Fulfilment Types
Physical Material; Progress-Based Work; Lump-Sum Work; Professional Service; Milestone; Commercial Adjustment; Omission.

## Claim Cycle
Progress Measurement → PCAR → CCAR → AR Invoice/Credit → Receipt/Allocation.

## Client vs Subcontract
PCAR ↔ Subcon Claim; CCAR ↔ Subcon Certificate; AR ↔ AP; Retention Receivable ↔ Retention Payable.
